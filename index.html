Common questions

How do I add a note?
Tap anywhere on the dotted canvas, type, and press Return. A small tick confirms it is saved.

How do I move or delete a note?
Drag a note to reposition it. Long-press and drag it onto the trash bin to delete it.

How do I see tomorrow's notes?
Use the arrows next to "Today", or open the calendar from the date pill.

How do I add the widget?
Long-press the Home Screen, tap +, search for Lazy Notepad and add the widget. Tapping it opens quick capture.

How do I sync my notes across devices?
Open your profile, choose Sign in with Apple, and make sure iCloud is on for Lazy Notepad.

How do I get more canvas colours?
Lazy Pro (.99 per month) unlocks every colour beyond Black. Use Restore Purchase on the paywall if you already subscribed.

How do I cancel Lazy Pro?
On your iPhone: Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Lazy Notepad > Cancel Subscription.
